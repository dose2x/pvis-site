Our Story

Why PVIS Exists

PVIS was founded in 2012 by Rachel Sunday after years in the employee benefits industry and seeing an opportunity to do things differently.

The goal wasn't to build the biggest brokerage. It was to build the kind of brokerage we believed employers deserved — knowledgeable, responsive, creative and genuinely invested in the people we serve.

Over the years, the industry has changed dramatically. Technology has changed. Compliance has become more complicated. Healthcare costs have continued to rise, and employers have more options — and more decisions — than ever before.

What hasn't changed is how we work.

  • We listen.
  • We ask questions.
  • We look for solutions.
  • We explain the complicated stuff.

And when we don't have the answer, we work with the right carrier, partner, compliance resource or ERISA attorney to get it.

That's still PVIS.

Leadership

Meet Rachel Sunday, Founder & CEO

Rachel Sunday founded Playa Vista Insurance Services in 2012 with a simple goal: build the kind of benefits brokerage she would want to work with.

With decades of experience in employee benefits, Rachel has built PVIS around relationships, responsiveness and a genuine curiosity about how businesses work. She believes the best benefits strategy starts by listening — understanding the company, its employees, its challenges and where it wants to go before recommending a solution.

Rachel is known for making complicated benefits issues understandable, asking a lot of questions, challenging the status quo when it makes sense, and being willing to say, “You don't need to change this,” when the solution a client already has is still the right one.

She also believes a broker's job extends well beyond insurance. From technology and compliance to HR resources, employee communication, wellness, cost management and long-term planning, Rachel has shaped PVIS to be the resource clients call when they need help — even when they aren't quite sure who they should be calling.

“I never wanted PVIS to be the biggest brokerage. I wanted us to be the brokerage our clients never wanted to leave.”

— Rachel Sunday, Founder & CEO

Outside of PVIS, Rachel brings that same enthusiasm to her family, community and a lifelong love of animals and wildlife conservation. She believes relationships matter — in business and in life — and that philosophy has shaped the culture of PVIS from the beginning.
