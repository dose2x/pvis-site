Small Businesses

Your Company Size Shouldn't Determine the Service You Receive.

Small employers have many of the same benefits challenges as larger organizations — just without a large HR department or benefits team to handle them.

We help small businesses understand their options, control costs, stay compliant and build benefits programs that make sense for their employees and their budget. And because smaller employers often need more guidance, not less, we're there to help throughout the year.

How we help

  • Benefits and plan options appropriate for your size and budget
  • Contribution and cost-management strategies
  • Benefits technology and enrollment support
  • Compliance guidance and resources
  • Employee education and support
  • Access to the same PVIS team throughout the year

Growing Businesses

Benefits That Grow With Your Business.

Growth changes things quickly. New employees, new locations, new states and new compliance requirements can turn a benefits program that once worked perfectly into something much more complicated.

PVIS helps growing employers think ahead — evaluating plan design, technology, administration, compliance and costs so your benefits program can evolve along with your company.

How we help

  • Plan design and contribution strategies that can evolve as you grow
  • Multi-state benefits considerations
  • Guidance as employee-count thresholds create new requirements
  • HR and benefits technology
  • Employee communication and enrollment
  • Benefits strategies that support recruiting and retention
  • Ongoing review as your needs change

Established Employers

A Fresh Look at What You Already Have.

And if what you have is working? We'll tell you that, too.

Who We Work With

Different Roles. One Benefits Partner.

Depending on the company, we may work with an HR professional, benefits team, CFO, business owner, office manager — or sometimes all of them. Our role changes depending on what you need from us.

HR & Benefits Professionals

Consider Us an Extension of Your Team.

HR already has enough on its plate. We help take benefits work off of it.

From employee questions and eligibility issues to enrollment, carrier coordination, technology, compliance resources and renewals, your PVIS team is there to help manage the details and solve problems as they come up.

You don't have to be the benefits expert. That's what we're here for.

Business Owners & Executives

Benefits Decisions Are Business Decisions.

Employee benefits are one of your largest investments in your people. We help owners and executives understand what they're spending, why they're spending it and whether their benefits strategy is supporting the business the way it should.

That means looking beyond the renewal increase to the bigger picture — cost, contribution strategy, recruiting and retention, employee experience, risk, compliance and long-term planning.

Our job is to give you the information and perspective you need to make a confident decision.

Employers Without a Dedicated HR Department

You Don't Need an HR Department to Have Great Benefits.

When benefits are being handled by an owner, office manager, controller or someone wearing five other hats, even a simple employee question can become time-consuming.

PVIS helps fill that gap. We provide the benefits knowledge, resources, employee support and hands-on assistance you may not have internally — while working alongside your payroll provider, HR resources, compliance professionals and other partners when needed.

You run your business. We'll help you navigate the benefits.
