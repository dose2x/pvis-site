Medical Insurance

More Than Choosing an HMO or PPO.

Medical coverage is typically the largest investment in an employer's benefits program — and there are more ways than ever to structure it.

PVIS evaluates the whole picture: cost, contributions, networks, employee demographics and needs, geography, plan design, funding options and risk. Then we help you understand the tradeoffs and determine which approach makes sense for your organization.

Medical solutions may include:

Plan types

  • HMO plans
  • PPO plans
  • HSA-qualified / high-deductible health plans

Funding arrangements

  • Fully insured plans
  • Level-funded plans
  • Self-funded arrangements

Strategies

  • Multiple-plan strategies
  • Alternative funding and cost-containment solutions

The newest or most complicated option isn't automatically the best one.

Voluntary Benefits

More Choice for Employees.

Voluntary benefits can give employees access to additional coverage based on their individual needs while allowing employers to expand the benefits available to their workforce.

PVIS helps evaluate which options make sense, how they fit alongside your core benefits and how they'll be communicated and administered.
