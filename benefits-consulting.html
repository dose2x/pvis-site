Benefits Strategy That Goes Beyond the Renewal.

Employee benefits touch nearly every part of your organization — your budget, your employees, recruiting and retention, HR, payroll, technology and compliance.

PVIS helps bring those pieces together. From strategy and plan design through implementation, enrollment and year-round support, we help you manage the whole benefits picture — not just your insurance policies.

Benefits Strategy

Start With the Business. Then Build the Benefits.

Before we recommend a plan, we want to understand your company.

  • What are you trying to accomplish?
  • What's working?
  • What's frustrating your employees?
  • Where are costs becoming a problem?
  • Are you growing?
  • Having trouble recruiting?
  • Is HR spending too much time administering benefits?

Then we build from there.

Our consulting may include

  • Plan design and funding strategy
  • Fully insured and alternative funding options
  • Contribution strategy and modeling
  • Carrier and market analysis
  • Cost-containment strategies
  • Benchmarking and competitive considerations
  • Recruiting and retention considerations
  • Multi-year benefits planning
  • Renewal strategy throughout the year

Sometimes better benefits means changing the plan. Sometimes it means changing how you pay for it, administer it or communicate it. And sometimes it means leaving a good plan alone.

Benefits create questions all year long.

  • An employee can't find their ID card.
  • A new hire needs coverage.
  • A termination didn't process correctly.
  • A bill doesn't match.

  • The enrollment system isn't behaving the way it should.

  • Claims assistance and escalation
  • Carrier and vendor coordination
  • Benefits technology support
  • Ongoing plan administration assistance

Open Enrollment

Open Enrollment Should Educate, Not Just Enroll.

Open enrollment isn't successful simply because everyone submitted an election before the deadline.

Employees should understand what they're choosing.

PVIS works with employers from planning through implementation to create an enrollment experience that is organized, understandable and appropriate for their workforce.

Our support may include

  • Open enrollment strategy and timeline
  • Employee communications and educational materials
  • Benefits guides and enrollment resources
  • In-person and virtual employee meetings
  • Benefits technology and enrollment support
  • Employee questions and decision support
  • Carrier and vendor coordination
  • Enrollment troubleshooting
  • Implementation and post-enrollment follow-up

The goal isn't just enrollment. It's helping employees understand and appreciate the benefits you're providing.

Compliance Support

You Don't Have to Navigate Benefits Compliance Alone.

Employee benefits come with an ever-changing list of rules, notices, deadlines and responsibilities. PVIS helps clients understand what applies to their benefits program, provides resources and guidance, and helps keep important requirements on the radar.

When something goes beyond the scope of benefits consulting, we help connect the dots — bringing in the appropriate compliance professional, TPA or ERISA counsel when needed.

Our support and resources may include

  • ACA guidance and reporting resources
  • COBRA coordination
  • ERISA resources and required notices
  • Section 125 / POP documentation
  • Form 5500 support and resources
  • SPD and plan document resources
  • HIPAA and benefits-related compliance guidance
  • Legislative and regulatory updates
  • Coordination with TPAs and compliance vendors
  • Access to experienced ERISA attorneys when legal guidance is needed
